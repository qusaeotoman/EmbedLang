# EmbedLang project continuity

- Preserve the verified compiler and firmware milestones. Explain necessary
  architecture changes before implementing them.
- After each completed milestone, update `docs/report/APPENDICES.md` and
  `docs/report/VERIFICATION.md` with the work and checks actually performed.
  Follow `docs/report/README.md`; do not invent dates, commits or test results.
- Keep the report in Russian. Preserve student-written text. The title page,
  technical requirements, personal architectural explanation and real AI-prompt
  history in `docs/report/REPORT.md` must be authored/reviewed by students;
  leave missing personal material explicitly marked rather than fabricating it.
- Distinguish local tests, simulated ARM/peripherals, ELF/BIN verification,
  programming verification and user-confirmed physical hardware observations.
- Do not claim compliance with an unavailable official assignment. Cite the
  actual assignment once supplied and identified.
- Do not publish, push or release new work without the user's approval for that
  publication. Keep generated artifacts, logs, credentials and machine-specific
  files out of Git. Do not access or program hardware without authorization.
